About me
I am a bioinformatician and computational biologist with a PhD in Bioinformatics from Leipzig University (2024, magna cum laude) and more than 12 years of experience across computational genomics, statistical genetics, and translational research.
I currently work as a Bioinformatician / Postdoctoral Researcher at the Helmholtz Institute for Translational Oncology (HI-TRON). My work includes multi-omics analysis, HPC-based workflows, cancer genomics, patient-level molecular reporting, and biomedical AI research.
Current research projects
1. Patient-specific integrated molecular oncology reports
Developing automated, interactive workflows that consolidate genomic and transcriptomic findings into structured patient-level research reports. The reporting interface covers somatic variants, mutational signatures, gene fusions, copy-number alterations, HLA typing, expression and pathway signals, immune-cell analyses, and candidate neoantigens.
Focus: R/Shiny, molecular oncology, reproducible analysis, interpretable reporting.
2. Multi-omics patient embedding and similarity modeling
Exploring integrated patient representations using genomic, transcriptomic, copy-number, immunophenotype, and clinical features. The research workflow compares new cases with reference cohorts, identifies molecularly similar patients, and examines possible associations with molecular subtypes and clinical outcomes.
Focus: TCGA, multi-omics integration, patient embeddings, dimensionality reduction, similarity analysis.
Outcome and risk prediction components are research concepts, not validated clinical tools.
3. Evidence-grounded AI for cancer variant interpretation
Investigating retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and biomedical AI agents for evidence-supported interpretation of molecular oncology findings. The approach retrieves information from resources such as ClinVar, CADD, CIViC, and OncoKB, then uses source-attributed evidence to support expert review.
Focus: RAG, biomedical knowledge resources, LLMs, provenance, variant interpretation.
AI-generated interpretations require expert validation.

Publishing with GitHub Pages
Create or open the GitHub repository named `Portfolio` under the account `akhilvelluva`.
Place `index.html`, this `README.md`, your profile photo, CV, and the `images/` folder in the repository root. Keep filenames and image paths unchanged.
