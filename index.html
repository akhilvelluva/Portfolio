First authorBMC Genomics · 2021Free web resource

PTEE: choosing the right tissue for RNA-seq diagnostics

The problem
In rare-disease genetics, the tissue that is clinically affected is often not the tissue that can be sampled, so RNA-seq can miss the signal that matters.
What I built
Phenotype-Tissue Expression and Exploration (PTEE), a freely available tool that links phenotype-annotated genes to expression across 54 GTEx tissues and shows which sampled tissue best represents the tissue of interest.
Why it helps
Clinical geneticists and researchers can choose a suitable tissue before sequencing, increasing the chance of a diagnosis or new gene discovery. PTEE also flags RNA-seq blind spots, such as genes switched on or off during development or by circadian rhythm.

Shared first author · equal contributionScience Advances · 2022Evolutionary genomics

Reading the genome of an extinct giant: Steller’s sea cow

The question
How did a giant marine mammal live in cold North Pacific waters, and what drove it to extinction?
What we found
Genomic analysis of the extinct Steller’s sea cow revealed the genetic basis of its distinctive skin and its adaptation to cold. Its population had been declining for tens of thousands of years before the species was first described.
Why it matters
The results suggest that environmental change, and not only human pressure, contributed to the extinction. This is a reference case for conservation genomics and for understanding how species adapt to extreme environments.

03 / Current research & engineering

Selected projects at HI-TRON.

Ongoing computational work connecting patient-level molecular reporting, spatial immune profiling, multi-omics analysis and evidence-grounded interpretation in translational oncology.

HI-TRON · OngoingMolecular oncology

Patient-specific integrated molecular oncology reports

Developing automated, interactive patient-level reporting workflows that bring heterogeneous genomic and transcriptomic analysis results into one interpretable interface for research and multidisciplinary review.

The attached reporting portal includes somatic variant summaries, mutational signatures, gene fusions, copy-number alterations, HLA typing, gene expression, pathway enrichment, immune transcriptional signatures, immune-cell deconvolution and neoantigen candidates. Patient and sample context is presented alongside molecular findings.

Purpose: Reduce fragmented analysis outputs and provide a reproducible, navigable basis for reviewing molecular evidence. This is a research reporting workflow, not an autonomous clinical decision system.

HI-TRON · Ongoing researchSpatial immuno-oncology

Spatial immune architecture & immunotherapy biomarker integration

Developing an analysis framework to characterize the tumor immune microenvironment from H&E and CD8-immunostained tissue images. Tissue and cell annotations support the extraction of quantitative spatial features, including CD8+ T-cell density, distance to tumor regions, neighboring CD8+ cells and tertiary lymphoid structure (TLS) organization.

Single-cell RNA-seq, where available, provides cell-type and cell-state context; when it is unavailable, immune-cell deconvolution of bulk RNA-seq can estimate cellular composition. These complementary measurements can be examined alongside genomic and transcriptomic biomarkers such as TMB, MSI and immune-expression signatures.

Research goal: Establish a reproducible foundation for spatial immunophenotyping and future multimodal integration to investigate tumor–immune interactions and potential immunotherapy-response biomarkers. This is exploratory research, not a clinically validated response-prediction tool.

HI-TRON · Research developmentMulti-omics & ML

Multi-omics patient embedding & similarity modeling

Exploring how harmonized genomic, RNA expression, copy-number, immunophenotype and clinical features can be integrated into patient representations, using large reference cohorts such as TCGA.

In the proposed workflow, a new patient is projected into the reference embedding, similar molecular profiles are identified and their clinical characteristics are summarized. Molecular subtype and outcome annotations support exploratory stratification and hypothesis generation.

Research goal: Build interpretable patient comparisons that may support biomarker discovery and downstream outcome modeling. The risk and survival prediction stages shown are conceptual and do not establish validated clinical performance.

HI-TRON · Ongoing researchBiomedical AI

Evidence-grounded AI for cancer variant interpretation

Investigating retrieval-augmented generation and biomedical AI agents to assist with the interpretation of somatic variants and molecular oncology questions.

The workflow brings together curated evidence from resources such as ClinVar, CADD, CIViC and OncoKB. Retrieved, source-attributed passages provide context for model-generated summaries, with emphasis on evidence provenance and reducing unsupported claims.

Research goal: Evaluate how retrieval, structured evidence and model reasoning can support transparent, reviewable molecular interpretation; outputs require expert validation.
