01 / About me

Bridging computation
and biomedical discovery.

I am a bioinformatics scientist and data engineer with more than 12 years of experience working across multi-omics, statistical genetics and translational research. My focus is building reliable analysis systems that make complex molecular information useful for scientific and clinical teams.

At the Helmholtz Institute for Translational Oncology (HI-TRON), my work includes integrative genomics, transcriptomics, spatial and clinical data analysis, reproducible HPC pipelines, patient-level molecular reporting and approaches for cancer biomarker discovery.

My earlier research at Leipzig University spanned rare disease genetics, RNA-seq-based clinical genetics, metabolic biology and evolutionary genomics.
